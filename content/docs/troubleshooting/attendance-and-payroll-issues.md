## Common Patterns
- Attendance looks incomplete or inconsistent.
- A payslip or 13th month record is expected but not visible.
- Biometric and attendance views do not appear to agree.

## What To Check First
- Confirm the correct date, period, or year.
- Confirm whether the payroll item may still be unreleased.
- Confirm whether the issue is a real mismatch or only a schedule or context misunderstanding.

## When To Escalate
Escalate when the difference has a real attendance, payroll, or compliance impact after the basic context checks are done.

## Common Patterns
- You are returned to the sign-in page unexpectedly.
- You are redirected to `Change Password` instead of your normal workspace.
- A page described in the docs does not appear in the sidebar or dashboard.

## What To Check First
- Confirm whether the session expired.
- Confirm whether the account currently requires a password update.
- Confirm whether the missing page depends on role or assigned capabilities.

## When To Escalate
Escalate when the account should legitimately have access, the same redirect behavior keeps repeating, or the missing page cannot be explained by role or workflow scope.

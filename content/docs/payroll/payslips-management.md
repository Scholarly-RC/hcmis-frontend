## What This Workspace Owns
- Reviewing payslips by employee and period.
- Creating or refreshing payroll records.
- Deciding when a payslip is ready to be visible to the employee.

## Release Is The Key Concept
A payslip can exist here before it is visible in the employee view. That difference is expected and should be treated as part of the payroll workflow.

## When Employees Report A Missing Payslip
First confirm whether the item has been released. Many “missing” cases are actually unreleased cases.

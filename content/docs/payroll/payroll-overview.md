## Two Sides Of Payroll
- Employee payroll pages are for viewing records that are already visible to the account.
- Administrative payroll pages are for creating, reviewing, adjusting, or releasing those records.

## Why A Payroll Record May Exist But Not Be Visible
Many payroll pages depend on a release step. A record can be present in payroll processing without yet appearing in the employee-facing view.

## Best Way To Navigate Payroll
Start with the question you need answered: “Can the employee view this yet?” is different from “Has payroll processing created or reviewed this record?”

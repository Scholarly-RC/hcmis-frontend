## What This Page Contains
Shared Resources holds documents or files that have been intentionally published to the current audience. Availability depends on access rules, not just the file existing in the system.

## If A File Is Missing
- The resource may not be published to your audience.
- The resource may have been removed or replaced.
- The resource may exist in the system but not be visible to your account.

## Best Next Step
If a resource is expected for your role but does not appear, ask the owner or administrator to confirm the publication and access scope rather than assuming the file upload failed.

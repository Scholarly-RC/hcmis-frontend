## What This Request Represents
Official business is a formal request workflow. It records an exception or planned activity that needs approval rather than changing attendance by assumption.

## What To Check On The Page
- Whether the request is still pending or already resolved.
- Whether the submitted details still match the intended business activity.
- Whether cancellation is still available in the current state.

## Common Misunderstanding
Approval status is the key indicator. If the request is approved but another downstream record still looks wrong, the next step is operational review rather than resubmitting the same request.

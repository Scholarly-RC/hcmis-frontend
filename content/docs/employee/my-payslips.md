## What Release Means
Payslips do not appear in the employee view as soon as payroll work starts. They become visible only after the payroll side makes them available for release.

## What To Check When A Payslip Is Missing
- Confirm the period or cutoff first.
- Confirm whether the issue is visibility rather than payroll creation.
- Escalate only after confirming that the record should already be released.

## What This Page Does Not Show
This page is for viewing and downloading released records. Administrative payroll work happens in separate payroll workspaces.

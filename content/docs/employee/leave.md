## What Matters Most
- A submitted leave request becomes part of the approval workflow.
- The current status tells you whether the request is still waiting, already resolved, or no longer actionable.
- Cancellation is usually only available while the request is still pending and allowed by the current workflow state.

## What To Review Before Submitting
- The requested date and leave type.
- Whether the request explanation is clear enough for review.
- Whether the request overlaps with a record you already submitted.

## Why A Request May Stay Pending
Pending means the request is still active in the workflow. It can be waiting on the assigned approver, a later stage, or an exception process handled outside the employee page.

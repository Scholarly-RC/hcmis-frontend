## What This Page Is For
Use this page when you need an attendance-related certification from the application workflow rather than a direct self-service record edit.

## What Reviewers Need
The request should make the target period or attendance issue clear enough for the reviewer to understand what is being certified.

## What To Expect After Submission
- The request enters the approval workflow.
- The status remains the main source of truth for whether action is still pending.
- Cancellation can be limited once review has already progressed.

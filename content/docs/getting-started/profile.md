## What Profile Includes
- Your visible account and identity details.
- Employment-related details that are exposed for viewing.
- Security controls such as password change.
- Training information when it is linked to your account.

## Editable Vs Managed Data
Not every field in Profile is self-service. Some values are controlled by HR-managed records, so they can appear read-only even though they are visible on the page.

## When To Use Another Process
If the change affects managed employment data, use the appropriate HR process instead of assuming the field can be edited directly from Profile.

## Core Concepts
- Attendance shows what HCMIS currently recognizes as work-related time data.
- Shifts, schedules, holidays, and punches provide the context for how that data is interpreted.
- Employee pages focus on one person’s view; HR pages support operational review across broader records.

## Why This Matters
Many attendance questions are not about broken pages. They are about how the system is interpreting incomplete, missing, or context-dependent records.

## Use This Guide Before Escalating
Confirm whether the issue is really a mismatch or only a misunderstanding of the shift, schedule, or current visible record.

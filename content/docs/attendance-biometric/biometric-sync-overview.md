## What This Workflow Does
- Compare application records with biometric-side records.
- Surface missing, matched, or mismatched users.
- Allow follow-up actions when the application can confidently proceed.

## What It Does Not Do
This page is not a full device deployment or infrastructure guide. It explains the application-facing sync and comparison workflow only.

## How To Read The Results
Matched records are usually informational. Mismatched or missing records are the ones that need review, especially when attendance or identity mapping depends on them.

## What Reconciliation Means
Reconciliation answers whether a record exists in the application, in the biometric source, or in both. The goal is to classify the mismatch before deciding on a corrective action.

## Typical Outcomes
- A biometric record exists but has no matching app user.
- The app user exists but is not properly matched to the biometric side.
- Both records exist, but the visible names or identifiers need review.

## Why Review Comes First
Automatic correction is not always safe. Reconciliation exists so administrators can confirm identity and record intent before they create, map, or adjust anything.

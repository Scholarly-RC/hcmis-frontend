## How To Add A Holiday
Open `Holiday Management`, select the add holiday action, enter the holiday name and date, choose whether it is recurring or year-specific, then save the record. Confirm the new holiday appears in the list for the selected year.

VIDEO: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1OE5ZFC53x9OVUB6cvJSQq9nnqUd2DYtm/view?usp=drive_link

## How To Edit A Holiday
Open `Holiday Management`, find the holiday you want to change, open the edit action, update the name, date, or year mode, then save the changes. Review the row after saving to confirm the updated values are reflected in the list.

VIDEO: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1KIO9ebrcOAmNUdl_6aL_7OdzLULKcmwB/view?usp=drive_link

## How To Delete A Holiday
Open `Holiday Management`, find the holiday you want to remove, open the delete action, and confirm the permanent delete prompt. Use this only when the holiday record is no longer needed for attendance rules.

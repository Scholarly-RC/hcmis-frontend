## How To Add A Department
Open `Department Management`, select the add department action, enter the department name and code, choose the active status, then save the record. Confirm the new department appears in the list with the expected code and status.

VIDEO: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Z_ywZ-wXPlcTwY5FBA70dHwGYOs69rf3/view?usp=drive_link

## How To Edit A Department
Open `Department Management`, find the department you want to change, open the edit action, update the name, code, or status, then save the changes. Review the row after saving to confirm the updated values are reflected in the list.

VIDEO: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1c-dzzmwCsSGO93TvbarfhEVKE1-qsTkS/view?usp=drive_link

## How To Activate Or Deactivate A Department
Find the department in `Department Management`, open the status action, and switch it to active or inactive as needed. Confirm the status badge changes after saving so the department matches the intended assignment state.

VIDEO: https://drive.google.com/file/d/17I21ttc8hIr2IHDlerWt75vK2Y0OQSnl/view?usp=drive_link

## How To Delete A Department
Find the department in `Department Management`, open the delete action, and confirm the permanent delete prompt. Use this only when the department is no longer needed and is not required by other records.

VIDEO: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1lX1jJSLFswhi0dvyAOrVRAsrV6knKvhT/view?usp=drive_link

## What HCMIS Covers
HCMIS brings common employee and HR workflows into one system. The main product areas are attendance, leave, overtime, official business, certificate requests, payroll visibility, approvals, and administrative workspaces.

## How To Use This Help Center
- Use `Employee Features` for self-service pages such as leave, overtime, attendance, payslips, or shared resources.
- Use `Approvals And Workflows` when the question is about status, approvers, or why a request is still pending.
- Use `HR And Admin Features` for operational pages used by HR, payroll, and administrators.
- Use `Attendance And Biometric` for attendance concepts, sync behavior, and mismatch interpretation.
- Use `Troubleshooting` when the issue is access, missing visibility, or a workflow that does not behave as expected.

## What These Guides Do Not Include
This help center explains user-facing workflows and business meaning. It does not include private recovery procedures, deployment instructions, device setup, or internal implementation details.
